I played this Pong game (python3 -m pong) and three things need fixing. 1) Where the ball hits the paddle changes nothing: the ball always leaves at the same angle, so rallies repeat. Hitting near the paddle's edge should send it off at a steeper angle, the centre flatter. 2) The paddles are drawn out of sync with their real position; they lag or jump on screen. 3) The screen flashes too much while playing. Keep everything else, keep the computer opponent, standard library only, and keep the unittest tests in tests/ passing, adding tests for the new paddle behaviour.
